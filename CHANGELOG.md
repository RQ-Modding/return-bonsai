# Changelog

Each section is a version of the mod, the `version` in `mod.json`. The release workflow copies the
section of a tagged version into the release notes.

## 1.1.0

Balance (Moru):
- Frostbuy's price is 50,000 gold every time (it doubled after each purchase, up to 1,000,000). A
  bonsai on the shelf from 1.0.0 at a higher price drops to 50,000 on the next visit.
- After a purchase, Frostbuy has another once quests are cleared: 3 in the first world, 2 in the
  second, none from the third (back on the next visit).

## 1.0.0

First version.
- The Return Bonsai, a held item: 2 cells of bonsai storage per bonsai worn on a quest's map, up to
  15, from a red map tab.
- A bonsai button on each Quest Storage tile, and on each bonsai storage tile to move it back.
- The bonsai storage's items stay in the storage however the quest ends, appraised for free.
- 3 quests with a map per bonsai, then Bonsai Ashes.
- Frostbuy: 50,000 gold, doubled each purchase up to 1,000,000, back after 3 quests cleared.
- A chest in the Malgrid Dungeon's 9th and 10th strata: 1%, once per run.
